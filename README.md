### 3.1 Your Statement of Intent

ValoUtil is an informative website that presents information on the mechanics, abilities and playstyles of different agents in Valorant. The site will be used to assist player's learning and understand the important mechanics and ideas that are essential to improve in Valorant. ValoUtil will contain a homepage with the icon of every Valorant agent, along with their names. It will also include a brief description of Valorant. This homepage will also contain a mechanic based area and a sub-branch page that covers the lore of Valorant storyline. If a specific agent icon is clicked on, the user will be transported to a page covering that agent's abilities, playstyle and their background lore. The abilities will feature their in-game icons, names and full descriptions, as well as extra information about hidden mechanics. These abilities will be located above the agents playstyle and lore. 

This website could create interest in Valorant, helping new players and veterans understand abilities, and provide them with guidance. Anyone interested in Valorant may use the website to increase their comprehension of Valorant and the website will be used frequently as a result of its functionality. The website will also contain the past patches made to specific agents in order to balance the game. This gives users the most recent, accurate information possible so that they may perform better and more efficiently. The website may also be used for players that are interested in the backstory of Valorant agents or the story the game surrounds. 

My website will help Valorant and hero shooter players understand abilities, mechanics, hidden techniques with abilities and the overall story/lore of the game. Users of my website will be able to improve their gameplay and appreciate the story of Valorant. Users can learn movement techs such as counter strafing, which involves tapping the opposite movement key to stop moving instantly and increase shooting accuracy,  specific lineups, wallbanging, peekers and angle advantage, one-way smokes, economy techniques, trading and playing on rounds where your in-game economy is suffering. 

My website is for people aged 13+, who are interested in first person hero shooters, and who are also interested in fast, complex fights, with difficult objectives. My website will be used by people who intend to get better at Valorant through understanding. These users will primarily be people who do not have the time to learn everything in game, with around 1 hour of time to play but intend to get better quickly. Players who have more time to spend or have already played Valorant for a while may be a secondary audience that could also benefit from using my website, as it may help them with agents they are new to or learning, and mechanics they struggle with properly learning. My website will help those who do not intend to learn mechanics, techniques and agents while playing, as it is a very time consuming process, and instead want to learn at an accelerated pace, without having to experience trial and error or waste time while playing.

ValoUtil will change how new and veteran players will learn, get better and rank up in Valorant, making it easier for people with less time to feel and perform at a higher level. With lore, abilities, vital mechanics, important techniques and necessary knowledge, I expect to help many people. This is because of the extensive tools I have created to help others, and the time I took to identify my target audience, purpose and how my website would help.
